About Wastechnology Computers

A journey of technical discipline, persistent innovation, and community commitment in Bwari, Abuja.

Founding Leadership

The Genesis of Wastechnology

Wastechnology was established by visionary technician and entrepreneur Ahmed Abdulwasiu. His keen curiosity for electronics and computational architecture emerged in early youth.

Training at Hot Cake Computers in his formative years, his natural diagnostic intuition quickly drew the admiration of both his mentors and patrons. By combining relentless problem-solving with transparent customer relations, he laid the foundations for what has grown into one of Bwari's foremost computer engineering hubs.

Growth Across Bwari

Tracing the operational milestones that shaped our present headquarters.

1. Along Sabon Gari Road

Formative years under the direct supervision of Mr. Musa. Gained invaluable mastery of customer dynamics, business administration, and workshop operations alongside peer colleague Abdulgafar (now CEO of RAMTECH).

2. Beside Bwari Area Council

Transitioned to an independent firm headquarters beside the Area Council Secretariat. Expanded hardware bench testing capacity and delivered timely computer solutions to local businesses and students.

3. Opposite First Bank Bwari

Operated for over two highly active years opposite First Bank. Steadily expanded our inventory of UK-used computers and component-level repair stations.

4. Glass Plaza (Current Headquarters)

For more than 4 years, Wastechnology Computers Engineering LTD has operated at Glass Plaza. This twin-facility site houses our reception and retail store on one side, and our fully equipped diagnostic repair center and Academy on the other.
